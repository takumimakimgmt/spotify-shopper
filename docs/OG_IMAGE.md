## Final generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: Open Graph social sharing cover for the real web app Playlist Shopper. Create one polished final raster cover in a wide 1200 x 630 composition, aspect ratio 1.905:1. The app compares Spotify playlists against a Rekordbox XML library and helps find purchase candidates on Beatport. Match the existing site's almost-black #04060a background, white clean sans serif type, restrained emerald green accents, subtle thin gray lines. This is a minimal editorial DJ music tool, confident and calm, not flashy sci-fi. Large readable exact text 'Playlist Shopper' as the main title. One smaller exact line 'Find missing tracks in Rekordbox.' and a discreet footer 'playlistshopper.com'. Integrate an elegant abstract visual of a short playlist meeting a record collection: a few crisp track rows, one understated green check, one open unfilled row, and partial vinyl grooves. All artwork and typography must fit comfortably in safe margins, no small interface text, no additional words, no third-party brand logos, no official affiliation implication, no prices, no gradients that reduce contrast, no fake app screenshot, no watermark. The title must remain legible as a small social card. Design should fill the whole frame as a flat finished graphic, no mockup framing, no device.
